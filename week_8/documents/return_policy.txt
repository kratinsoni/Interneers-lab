RETURN POLICY — BuildRight Toys Inc.
Effective Date: January 2025 | Policy Version 5.1

======================================================
SECTION 1: STANDARD RETURN POLICY
======================================================

General Return Window:
Customers may return most products within 30 days of the original purchase date for a full
refund. Items must be in original, unused condition with all original packaging, manuals,
and accessories included. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7
business days of receiving the returned item.

Extended Holiday Returns:
Items purchased between November 1 and December 31 may be returned until January 31 of
the following year. This extended window applies to both in-store and online purchases.

Non-Returnable Items:
The following items cannot be returned under any circumstances:
- Opened consumable/chemical science kits (due to safety regulations)
- Digital download codes once redeemed
- Customized or personalized products
- Items marked "Final Sale" at the time of purchase
- Gift cards and store credit vouchers

======================================================
SECTION 2: RETURNS FOR DAMAGED OR DEFECTIVE ITEMS
======================================================

Damaged on Arrival (DOA):
If your item arrives visibly damaged (crushed box, broken pieces visible through packaging),
do NOT accept the delivery if possible. If already accepted:
1. Photograph all damage within 24 hours of delivery.
2. Contact customer support within 48 hours at support@buildright.com or 1-800-555-BUILD.
3. Include your order number, photos, and a brief description of the damage.
4. BuildRight will arrange a prepaid return label at no cost to the customer.
5. A full replacement or refund will be issued within 3–5 business days of receiving the
   damaged item back at our warehouse.

Defective Items (Post-Use):
If a product becomes defective during normal use within the warranty period:
1. Stop using the product immediately if there is a safety concern.
2. Contact our warranty team at warranty@buildright.com with proof of purchase and a
   description of the defect.
3. Do not attempt to repair the item yourself, as this voids the warranty.
4. BuildRight reserves the right to repair, replace, or refund at our discretion.
5. Shipping costs for defective item returns are covered by BuildRight for items under warranty.

Damage Due to Misuse:
Returns will NOT be accepted for damage resulting from:
- Accidental drops or physical impact not related to a manufacturing defect.
- Water or liquid damage (unless the product is marketed as waterproof).
- Modification or tampering with the product.
- Normal wear and tear beyond the warranty period.
- Use outside of the age range or operating instructions specified in the product manual.

Partial Returns / Missing Pieces:
If you received a set with missing pieces that were not caused by your use, contact
support within 14 days of purchase. We will ship replacement pieces free of charge.
If the number of missing pieces exceeds 10% of the total set, a full replacement will
be issued instead.

======================================================
SECTION 3: EXCHANGE POLICY
======================================================

Size / Color Exchanges:
Exchanges for different variants of the same product are accepted within 30 days.
The original item must be returned unopened. Exchange shipping is free for the first
exchange; subsequent exchanges may incur a $5.99 shipping fee.

Upgrade Exchanges:
Customers may exchange a product for a higher-priced item within 30 days by paying the
price difference. Downgrade exchanges follow the standard refund policy.

======================================================
SECTION 4: REFUND METHODS
======================================================

Original Payment Method:
Refunds are returned to the original payment method in all standard cases. Credit card
refunds appear within 5–7 business days. Debit card and bank transfer refunds may take
up to 10 business days.

Store Credit:
At the customer's request, refunds may be issued as store credit (valid for 1 year).
Store credit refunds are processed immediately upon receipt of the returned item.

Gift Returns:
Items received as gifts may be returned for store credit with a gift receipt. Without a
gift receipt, store credit will be issued for the current selling price of the item.

======================================================
SECTION 5: HOW TO INITIATE A RETURN
======================================================

Online Returns:
1. Log in to your account at buildright.com.
2. Navigate to "Order History" and select the relevant order.
3. Click "Initiate Return" and follow the prompts.
4. Print the prepaid shipping label (for eligible returns) and drop off at any UPS location.

In-Store Returns:
Bring the item, original receipt or order confirmation email, and all original packaging
to any BuildRight retail location. In-store returns are processed immediately.

Customer Support Contact for Returns:
Phone: 1-800-555-BUILD (Mon–Fri, 8 AM–6 PM EST)
Email: returns@buildright.com
Live Chat: buildright.com/support
