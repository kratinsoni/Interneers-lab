VENDOR FAQ — BuildRight Toys Inc.
Partner & Supplier Resource Document | Version 2.0 | January 2025

======================================================
SECTION 1: BECOMING A VENDOR
======================================================

Q: How do I apply to become a vendor/supplier for BuildRight Toys?
A: Interested vendors should submit a Vendor Application Form available at
buildright.com/vendor-portal. Applications require: company registration documents,
proof of product liability insurance ($2M minimum coverage), product safety certifications
(ASTM F963, EN71, or equivalent), and three business references. Applications are reviewed
within 15 business days.

Q: What product categories is BuildRight currently sourcing?
A: We are actively sourcing in the following categories: construction sets and blocks,
outdoor and active play equipment, STEM and educational kits, plush and soft toys,
arts and crafts supplies, and electronic learning toys. We are not currently accepting
new vendors for board games or puzzles as those categories are at capacity.

Q: Is there a minimum order quantity (MOQ) requirement?
A: Yes. BuildRight requires a minimum order of 500 units per SKU for initial purchase
orders. Repeat orders after a 6-month performance review may qualify for reduced MOQ of
250 units per SKU, subject to category manager approval.

Q: Does BuildRight offer exclusivity agreements?
A: Exclusivity agreements are available for premium vendor partners who meet our Platinum
Tier requirements (minimum $500K annual supply value, 98%+ on-time delivery, <0.5% defect
rate). Exclusivity is granted on a per-category basis for a 12-month renewable term.

======================================================
SECTION 2: PRICING AND PAYMENT TERMS
======================================================

Q: What are BuildRight's standard payment terms?
A: Standard payment terms are Net-45 from the date of invoice. Vendors who pass a
creditworthiness review may qualify for Net-60 terms. Early payment discounts of 2% are
available for invoices paid within 10 days (2/10 Net-45).

Q: How does BuildRight handle price increases from vendors?
A: Vendors must provide a minimum of 90 days written notice before any price increase.
Price increases greater than 5% must be approved by our procurement committee and may
trigger a re-bidding process. Emergency cost increases due to raw material shortages
are evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

Q: What currency does BuildRight process vendor invoices in?
A: All domestic vendor invoices must be in USD. International vendors may invoice in
their local currency, but payment will be made in USD at the exchange rate on the
invoice date. BuildRight does not hedge currency for individual vendor transactions.

Q: How do vendors submit invoices?
A: All invoices must be submitted through the Vendor Portal at buildright.com/vendor-portal.
Paper invoices are no longer accepted as of January 2024. Invoices must include: purchase
order number, itemized line items with SKU codes, unit prices, and applicable taxes.

======================================================
SECTION 3: SHIPPING AND LOGISTICS
======================================================

Q: What are BuildRight's receiving warehouse locations?
A: Our primary distribution centers are located in Columbus, OH (East Coast fulfillment),
Dallas, TX (South and Central), and Reno, NV (West Coast fulfillment). All shipments must
be directed to the DC specified on the purchase order. Mis-shipped goods may be refused
or subject to a $250 rerouting fee.

Q: What packaging standards must vendors comply with?
A: All products must comply with BuildRight Packaging Standard BRS-2024, available on the
Vendor Portal. Key requirements: master cartons must be double-walled corrugated, weight
per carton must not exceed 30 kg, barcodes must be GS1-compliant, and all cartons must
include country of origin labeling. Non-compliant packaging may result in a chargeback
of $150 per pallet.

Q: What lead times does BuildRight expect?
A: Standard lead times for domestic vendors: 10–15 business days from PO confirmation.
International vendors: 45–60 days including customs clearance. Rush orders (less than
7 business days) incur a 15% surcharge and are subject to capacity availability.

Q: Who arranges freight for inbound shipments?
A: BuildRight uses a Vendor Collect freight program for domestic suppliers. Our logistics
team will schedule pickup via our preferred carrier (FedEx Freight or UPS Freight) and
deduct freight costs from vendor payment per the agreed freight allowance. International
vendors are responsible for arranging FOB shipments to the designated port of entry.

======================================================
SECTION 4: QUALITY AND COMPLIANCE
======================================================

Q: What quality certifications do vendors need?
A: All toy vendors must hold current ASTM F963 (US) and EN71 (EU if applicable)
certifications. Electronic toys additionally require FCC Part 15 and CE marking. Chemical
kits require CPSC compliance documentation. Certifications must be renewed annually and
uploaded to the Vendor Portal.

Q: What happens if a shipment fails BuildRight's quality inspection?
A: BuildRight inspects 10% of units in each shipment using AQL Level II sampling.
If the defect rate exceeds 1.5%, the entire shipment is rejected and returned at the
vendor's expense. The vendor must provide a corrective action plan (CAP) within 10
business days. Three rejections within a 12-month period will result in vendor
suspension pending a performance review.

Q: Does BuildRight conduct factory audits?
A: Yes. All new vendors undergo an initial factory audit conducted by a BuildRight-approved
third-party auditor (cost borne by the vendor, approximately $800–$1,500 depending on
location). Ongoing audits occur annually for Tier 1 suppliers and bi-annually for Tier 2.
Unannounced spot audits may be conducted if quality or compliance concerns arise.

======================================================
SECTION 5: VENDOR PERFORMANCE & PARTNERSHIP
======================================================

Q: How is vendor performance measured?
A: Vendors are scored quarterly on four KPIs: On-Time Delivery Rate (target: 95%+),
Order Fill Rate (target: 98%+), Defect Rate (target: <1%), and Invoice Accuracy (target:
99%+). Scores are shared via the Vendor Portal dashboard. Vendors falling below targets
for two consecutive quarters enter a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).

Q: How do I escalate a dispute about a deduction or chargeback?
A: Submit a dispute via the Vendor Portal within 30 days of the deduction appearing on
your remittance. Include supporting documentation (BOL, photos, emails). Disputes are
reviewed by our Vendor Relations team within 15 business days. Unresolved disputes may
be escalated to the VP of Procurement for final review.

Q: Who is my main contact at BuildRight?
A: Each vendor is assigned a dedicated Vendor Relations Manager (VRM). Your VRM's
contact information is listed in the Vendor Portal under "My Account." For general
inquiries, contact vendor.relations@buildright.com.
