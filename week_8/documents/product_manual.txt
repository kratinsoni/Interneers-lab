SECTION 1: LEGO CASTLE DELUXE SET (Model #BR-7821)
======================================================

Overview:
The Lego Castle Deluxe Set is designed for children aged 9 and above. It contains 1,842 pieces
and includes 8 minifigures (4 knights, 2 archers, 1 king, and 1 queen). The set measures
38 cm × 52 cm × 28 cm when fully assembled.

Warranty:
The Lego Castle Deluxe Set comes with a 2-year manufacturer warranty covering defects in
materials and workmanship. The warranty period begins from the date of purchase. Warranty
claims must be submitted within 30 days of discovering the defect. Proof of purchase is
required for all warranty claims.

Assembly Instructions:
- Read all instructions before beginning assembly.
- Sort pieces by color and size before starting.
- Do not force pieces together; if resistance is felt, verify you have the correct pieces.
- Estimated assembly time: 6–8 hours.
- Adult supervision recommended for children under 12.

Safety Warnings:
- Small parts present. Not suitable for children under 3 years.
- Keep away from heat sources; pieces may warp at temperatures above 60°C.
- Do not expose to prolonged direct sunlight; may cause discoloration.

Replacement Parts:
Missing or broken parts can be requested free of charge within the warranty period by
contacting our parts replacement hotline: 1-800-555-LEGO. Outside the warranty period,
individual parts may be purchased at buildright.com/parts at $0.05–$2.00 per piece depending
on size and complexity.

SECTION 2: REMOTE CONTROL RACING CAR (Model #BR-3310)
======================================================

Overview:
The RC Racing Car is suitable for ages 6 and above. It features a 2.4 GHz remote with a
control range of up to 30 meters. Maximum speed is 25 km/h. Battery life is approximately
45 minutes per charge (USB-C charging, full charge in 90 minutes).

Warranty:
The RC Racing Car includes a 1-year limited warranty. The warranty covers manufacturing
defects but does not cover damage from crashes, water exposure, or unauthorized modifications.
Battery degradation is not covered under warranty after 6 months of use.

Operating Instructions:
- Charge the car fully before first use.
- The remote requires 2 × AA batteries (not included).
- Frequency: 2.4 GHz — safe to operate alongside other RC devices.
- Press the SYNC button on both the car and remote simultaneously to pair.

Maintenance:
Clean the car with a dry cloth after each use. Do not use water or chemical solvents.
Inspect wheels and axles monthly for wear. Replacement wheels available at buildright.com.

SECTION 3: EDUCATIONAL SCIENCE KIT (Model #BR-5500)
======================================================

Overview:
The Educational Science Kit targets ages 8–14 and includes materials for 25 experiments
covering chemistry, physics, and biology. All chemicals included are non-toxic and
approved by the ASTM F963 safety standard.

Warranty:
The Educational Science Kit has a 90-day warranty on physical components (goggles, trays,
measuring tools). Consumable chemical supplies are not covered under warranty. Any equipment
that arrives damaged must be reported within 7 days of delivery.

Storage & Safety:
- Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
- Keep all chemicals out of reach of children when not in supervised use.
- Do not mix chemicals outside of the provided experiment instructions.
- Dispose of used chemicals in accordance with local regulations.
